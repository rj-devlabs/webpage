# 🥔 Potato Driver

**One potato. One steering wheel. Absolutely no idea where the turn signal is.**

Welcome to the official repository for **Spudley**, a potato with a driver’s license, a little red car, and a dream of seeing the whole world one questionable road trip at a time.

## Meet Spudley

Spudley was once an ordinary potato sitting quietly in a pantry. Then one day, a tiny toy car rolled past. Spudley watched it go by and thought, “I could do that.”

After several weeks of practicing with a steering wheel made from a bagel, Spudley passed the driving test. The examiner asked Spudley to perform a three-point turn. Spudley performed a three-point turn, a four-point turn, and one accidental spin around the parking lot.

The examiner was impressed by the confidence and slightly concerned by the bagel steering wheel.

Now Spudley cruises the countryside in a bright red car named **Mash-mobile**. The passenger seat is reserved for a small toolbox, an emergency snack, and a map that Spudley insists is “only a little bit upside down.”

## Spudley’s vehicle

The Mash-mobile is a compact red car with:

- Four wheels and one potato-sized driver’s seat
- A horn that sounds like a squeaky kitchen sponge
- A dashboard covered in tiny stickers
- A cup holder for juice, gravy, or other road-trip beverages
- A glove compartment containing three maps, a spare hat, and a mysterious pebble
- An emergency brake that Spudley takes very seriously

For especially ambitious adventures, Spudley sometimes drives a **colossal moon-powered tractor the size of a mountain**. It has 900 wheels, a turn signal visible from space, and enough room in the cab for Spudley, the entire potato family, and several very confused sheep.

The tractor is not street legal on Earth, the Moon, or anywhere else. Spudley says this is “a paperwork problem.”

## Favorite destinations

Spudley enjoys driving to places that are easy to park near and have good snacks:

1. **The Garden Shed** — excellent views, questionable road surfaces.
2. **The Picnic Hill** — a perfect spot for a lunch break and a nap.
3. **The Big Roundabout** — Spudley once drove around it 17 times and called it sightseeing.
4. **The County Fair** — Spudley loves the bumper cars, although technically they are not supposed to be driven onto the road.
5. **The Moon** — reachable only in the mountain-sized tractor, and only when the moon-powered engine feels cooperative.

## Road-trip rules

Spudley follows a strict set of driving rules:

- Always wear a tiny seat belt.
- Never drive faster than a rolling onion.
- Stop for snacks when the snack alarm goes off.
- Use the turn signal, even when no one is around.
- Do not argue with the GPS. It has a very confident voice.
- If the road ends, stop the car and reconsider the plan.
- Never let the passenger eat the emergency snack without permission.

Spudley is an excellent driver, but a somewhat unpredictable navigator. “Left” sometimes means “the other left,” and “almost there” could mean anywhere between one minute and three counties away.

## Getting started

Want to follow Spudley’s adventures?

1. Clone this repository.
2. Open the website.
3. Read about the latest potato-powered road trip.
4. Keep your hands, feet, and vegetables inside the vehicle.

## Safety note

Spudley is a fictional potato. Real potatoes should not drive cars, tractors, bicycles, shopping carts, or anything else that requires a license. If you find a potato near your car keys, please put the keys somewhere safe and offer the potato a nice bowl instead.
